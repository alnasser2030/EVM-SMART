External Energy Flexibility

A smart-grid supply framework for inflexible AI data centers
Ali Nasser · PhD Resident

The data center takes 100% of its energy from the grid and uses none of its solar. The solar is sold to the grid and runs flexible loads at noon; at night those loads step back by as much as the data center uses. Hour by hour, the room they leave on the grid carries the data center.

Where it works, and why 90%

8 MW–1.5 GWdata-center size; larger = groups of campuses
  1. Highest target the same solar plant can reach: solar equal to annual use with daily shifting tops out at 91.2%.
  2. First target that frees almost all summer evenings (99.2%), so the data center adds no night peak.
  3. Costs about the same as lower targets: +US$0.3/MWh from 80% to 90%, while emissions halve.
  4. 100% is out of reach: storage losses, dusty days and hour order cap it near 99.9%.
